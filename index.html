Mara Reih Liata Prefix (Bie hmia abaipa)

Prefix cha khâpa maw? & Chi khazie y?
Biesâ thokha cha a hmia lâta biesâ hropa baipa ta bie yzie hropa hneipa lâta a pua tyh. Mara reih liata eima hmâ viapazy cha A-, Pa-, Apa- zy he ama châ via chai.
Root word liata A, PA, APA abai chhieliepazy deikua Prefix hawhta pachâpa châ veih ei (Pamosana: Atahmâ, atanoh, arâ, Pasu, Patu, Apara, Apalai etc.). Mara reih liana heta bie hmia lâta abaipa (prefixes) peimawhpa, eima châ ropa achhuahna, hmo taozie palâsatuhpa nata dytuhpa palâsatuhpa nano nanopa y ta. Chazy cha reciprocal, reflexive, nata causative prefixes zy ama châ via chai.
1. Reciprocal Prefix (Atah veichâhna / Avei rairiena)
Prefix: A- (each other / one another)
• Kyhpachâ -> Kyh apachâ (Love each other/love one another). 'A' pahla khiahta chyhsa pakha ta mo hropa kyh a kei pachâna a dy ha aw. • Bao -> Abao (Akhokhei veichâh / adeichhy veichâhna). 'A' pazao leipa khiahta chyhsa pakha ta mo hropa a kei baona kyh reina a châ ha.
2. Reflexive Prefix (Sasyh atahna / Sasyh chô liata hmo taopa)
Prefix: A- (himself / herself / myself)
• Pachha -> Apachha (Sasyh apabohsana tlyma a dy). 'A' pahla deikua khiahta chyhsa pakha ta mo hropa a pachhapa reina a châ ha aw. Context (bie siezie) ta pahnothai theipa a châ.
3. Causative Prefix (Pa-ysatuhpa / Patlôsatuhpa)
Prefix: Apa nata Pa (to cause someone to do something / make something happen)
• Rao -> Parao (Hmo raona tlôkheina palâsana) • Sia -> Apasiasa (Sasyh sia atahna palâsana) (Mara reih liata bie hnôh lâta abaipa 'suffix' eima reih heih aw)

Prefix & Suffix

Prefix
Word beginnings attached before a root or stem to modify meaning, grammatical role, or verb morphology in Mara.
Suffix
Word endings attached after a root or stem to mark tense, mood, plurality, question, or relation (e.g., -aw, -ma, -pa, -zy).

Parts of Speech

Noun
Names of persons, places, things, or ideas (e.g., O, Khih, Ti, Chyhsa).
Pronoun
Words used in place of nouns (e.g., Kei / Ei, Nâ, Ano, Eimâ).
Verb
Action or state words; Mara verb forms shift with stem verbs, prefixes, and suffixes.
Adjective
Describes or qualifies nouns (e.g., pha, sâh, chhie).
Adverb
Modifies verbs, adjectives, or manner/frequency (e.g., kaw, lymâ, châ).
Postposition, Conjunction & Particle
Shows place, direction, relation, or connects clauses/phrases (e.g., liata, tawhta, nata, tlâ, Uh).

Mara Reih Liata H Nata Â

Pronunciation of 'A' & 'Â' (Mara reih liata A nata Â parosazie)
In the Mara language, the circumflex accent (â) indicates a higher tone (upper tone). "a" (without accent) = lower tone; "â" (with circumflex accent) = higher tone.
Mara reih liata cha Â luhkhupa heta aony asâh viapa, aony asâhpa ta parosa awpa tahpa pamosa ta. A — like 'u' in 'Sun'; Â — like 'a' in 'Father'. Common Language Tlôsaih reih parosana chôta roh awpa; 'H' bai awpa nata bai leina (pamosana: Châ ei roh / Châ rona bu, Ei pyh / apypa, Ei khoh / Khona, Eimâ tlyh / Atlyna, Pavaw ei siah / siapa, Film eima moh / mo pha kawpa; Rôhnah, Hmeihseih, Khihnahrâh; Hriakhohna, Khaihna, Palôhrupa, Angiahna; Râhhmâhpa, Lyurâhhripa). Noun liata 'H' bai leh 'Â', 'Ô' hmâna (Beicho/Beichô, Chavah/Chava, Pasâhsa/Pasasa, Zotha/Zôthâh, Pawzô/Pawzoh). 'Â' hmâna: Âchhyna-O, Â vy mah y, Âhneina, Âkhohna, Ângia, Ârâpasua vs. Â ngia kaw, Â râ haw, Kho â pao kaw, Â dopa ta tao teh (Prefix châta Â hmâna a sôh kaw).

Orthography, Tone & Spacing Rules

1. EI CHÂ LY ✅️ / EI CHÂLY ❌️
Write as three separate words (EI Subject + CHÂ Object + LY Verb), never 'EI CHÂLY'.
2. CHA PIE NATA CHAPIE
'CHA PIE' = Pronoun + Verb (Phusâ ei cha pie, Khazohpa ta byhnâ cha pie mawh sy, Châbu ei cha pie aw); 'CHAPIE' = standalone Verb (Football ei chapie, A khi ei chapie, A chaotô liata ei chapie).
3. AW NATA AWH
'AW' = Future Tense / Verb (Ei tao aw, Ei nie aw, Ei sie aw, Lailai kha vaw aw tua); 'AWH' = Noun or Verb with 'H' (Awhkhypa; Tlâh ama awh, Athata awh tyh).
4. "MY PHA" tahpa he khazia eima pazao vei
MY PHA is a Phrase (My = Noun, Pha = Adjective — daihti phapa hmâh awpa khokheina), not a close compound word like 'hâhchatana' or 'pinoh'.
